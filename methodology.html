Topic and Methodology

Selected topic

The project studies Icona agiografica – San Caralampo, an eighteenth-century Russian icon held in Brescia, Italy. Its ArCo record describes a rectangular religious painting in tempera on wood.

Research methodology

  1. Search for Russian icons using SPARQL and identify the selected record.
  2. Compare the direct description and historical-information properties with other icon records to identify gaps.
  3. Test ChatGPT and Perplexity with identical zero-shot, few-shot and step-by-step prompts.
  4. Compare the information, sources and limitations of the responses.
  5. Design proposed RDF triples using ArCo properties and identify the verification still required.

Tools and data sources

  • ArCo knowledge graph
  • Italian Ministry of Culture SPARQL endpoint
  • ChatGPT and Perplexity
  • Italian cultural heritage catalogue, SIGECweb and academic literature cited in the LLM experiment
  • SPARQL, RDF and GitHub Pages
