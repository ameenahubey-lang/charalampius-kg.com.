Designing Triples

Based on these findings, I proposed two RDF triples using the existing ArCo properties core:description and a-cd:historicalInformation.

1. core:description — Iconographic composition

@prefix core: <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/core/> .

@prefix obj: <https://w3id.org/arco/resource/HistoricOrArtisticProperty/> .

obj:0303280124

core:description

"A rectangular Russian icon painted in tempera on wood. Saint Charalampius is depicted frontally in the central panel, with long hair and a beard, wearing a blue embroidered vestment. He raises his right hand in blessing and holds a scroll in his left hand. Narrative scenes from his life surround the central figure. The scroll contains a prayer for protection from famine and pestilence."@en .

2. a-cd:historicalInformation — Historical background

@prefix a-cd: <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/context-description/> .

@prefix obj: <https://w3id.org/arco/resource/HistoricOrArtisticProperty/> .

obj:0303280124

a-cd:historicalInformation

"According to Christian hagiographical tradition, Saint Charalampius was an early Christian martyr associated with Magnesia in Asia Minor. He became widely venerated in Orthodox Christianity as a protector against plague and other epidemics. The growth of his cult during plague outbreaks contributed to the development of more detailed narrative images of his martyrdom in post-Byzantine art. The eighteenth-century Russian icon in Brescia belongs to this wider iconographic tradition."@en .

Overall, the experiment shows that LLMs can help researchers find information for knowledge graph enrichment. However, their answers should be checked against reliable sources before the proposed triples are accepted.
