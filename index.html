01 / PROJECT

Abstract

This project explores how SPARQL and large language models can support the enrichment of ArCo, the Italian cultural heritage knowledge graph. We focus on an eighteenth-century Russian icon of Saint Charalampius in Brescia. We examine its record, identify information gaps, compare responses from ChatGPT and Perplexity, and propose additional RDF statements.

Team: Marina Khamokova

Object: Icona agiografica – San Caralampo, 0303280124

02 / PROCESS

04 / FINDINGS

Information Gaps

Gap 1: Historical information

Our comparison query returned no value for the direct property a-cd:historicalInformation on the selected icon. We therefore explored sources that could provide relevant historical context.

Gap 2: Iconographic description

The existing core:description gives basic information about the material, shape, artistic tradition, and religious subject. It does not explain the composition or how the saint is represented. A fuller description could include the central figure, gestures, clothing, scroll, and surrounding scenes.
